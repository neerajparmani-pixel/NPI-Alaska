# Screenshot and automated audit

Local fixture data; no production session. See RELEASE-AUDIT.md one folder above for scope, limits and live acceptance.

1. **Sign in** — Approved split desktop layout, transparent company emblem and company account form.
2. **NPI overview** — Oversight navigation, review counts and actionable operational summary.
3. **Work register** — Search, filters, creator and assigned team are visible. Searches are server-paginated.
4. **Assignment and team** — Attendance, assigned workers and evidence share one work record.
5. **Private gallery** — Before and after evidence is grouped by stage. Training camera images are synthetic.
6. **Stamped viewing copy** — Time-labelled preview and unchanged original link are separate.
7. **NPI recipients** — Organisation-scoped email settings and escalation deadline.
8. **Company issue** — Owner, status, resolution and comments retained with the work.
9. **Worker home** — Single-column phone layout, site photo, prominent assignment action and four bottom links.
10. **Multiple upload queue** — Two prepared photos, individual removal and retry-safe batch submission.
11. **Worker problem report** — Simple category, urgency and description; critical danger instruction visible.
12. **Direct camera** — Capture, keep and retake controls; repeated captures stay in one queue.
13. **Submission confirmation** — Explicit success dialog explains the next company review step.
14. **Exception verification** — Incomplete-work acknowledgement is separate from accepting or adjusting hours.
